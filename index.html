The Agnostic Layer

Nine assumptions this system never made

Nine axes, each specific about what it refuses to assume. The substrate switch above is the smallest live example.

Host The machine is probed, never assumed.

A stdlib-only, stateless capability detector reports what's actually present: NVIDIA, AMD, Apple Silicon, Intel, or CPU alone. The inference backend then resolves CUDA → Vulkan → CPU and takes the first one the hardware can genuinely use. Vulkan is the deliberate middle rung, because one Vulkan build covers all three vendors with no runtime libraries to chase.

Operating system No branch on platform.

pathlib throughout, no shell-outs to platform binaries, and process supervision behind a single interface. POSIX and NT take the same code path. Where a filename genuinely differs, both are probed rather than one assumed.

Location Nothing knows where it lives.

One module answers "where does this live" for the whole system: ARCHEMEDEUS_HOME, plus a per-area override for runtime state, residents, models, and binaries. Every path resolves at call time rather than at import. Move the tree, point an environment variable at it, and nothing downstream notices.

Model Identity is not in the weights.

Residents live in authored layers: a soul document, a voice file, and a constitutional role. Swap the base model out and the resident survives intact, because the weights were only ever infrastructure.

Provider No pinned vendor.

A health-checked provider pool admits anything that satisfies the interface. Local inference and hosted APIs are peers, and a dead provider degrades the pool instead of the system.

Surface Terminal, chat, and browser are the same.

One Shell Contract, four implementations. The web shell gets no special-casing for being a browser. If a surface needs an exception, the contract was wrong.

Script The writing system is a surface, not a limit.

Baybayin is a first-class script here rather than an encoding exception. Likha is written entirely in it and still transpiles to ordinary Python. Unicode identifiers and UTF-8 boundaries are tested explicitly rather than assumed to work.

Network Offline is the posture, not the fallback.

The PWAs cache first and reconcile later; the LAN tools are standard-library only and expect no internet at all. Connectivity is an upgrade, never a precondition.

State Memory travels as envelopes.

Continuity is serialized, hash-chained, and portable by construction. Anything that can carry bytes can carry the house, and nothing becomes authoritative without passing an owner gate.

Agnostic is not the same as generic. Everything above is specific, and always refuses to be specific about the wrong things.

Not yet true

Two axes haven't been earned yet. They're listed because a doctrine that only records its wins stops being a doctrine, and because the gap is the next piece of work rather than an embarrassment.

Storage backend open Persistence is JSONL, and only JSONL.

Every durable store is append-only JSONL. That is a deliberate choice for auditability and hash-chaining, not an accident, but there is no common interface behind it, so swapping in a database is not yet a config line. The location is abstracted. The backend is not.

Language reach open Zyeha isn't callable from anywhere else yet.

The plan is one C-ABI export surface rather than a binding per language, since every host language already knows how to call C. The numeric model and string semantics were chosen to stay compatible with that boundary, but compatibility with a future boundary is not the boundary.

The Prime Law
  1. Exploration is free.
  2. Adoption is governed.
  3. Protected consequence is approved.

Looking costs nothing, so the system is allowed to be curious. Changing what persists needs a rule. Anything that touches something protected needs a person. Everything further down this page is a consequence of those three lines.

Why the namesZyeha, Archemedeus, Arcaia — the etymology behind all three

Why Zyeha

Unlike Archemedeus and Arcaia, this one borrows nothing. The meaning is self-authored and structural. Z opens the name; A closes it. The alphabet's last letter placed first, its first letter placed last. So the name doesn't describe continuity, it performs it: an ending used as a beginning, a beginning arriving disguised as an ending. Read as a shape rather than a word, it's a loop that never quite closes, the end feeding back into being a start again.

The sense underneath is one idea from four angles — start, origin, continuous, continuity: a beginning that doesn't stop being a beginning, an origin that keeps originating instead of receding into the past once something has been built on it. Which is a literal description of what the language is for, and of the pen name it shares: the continuous origin of Light. The two disciplines arrived at the same name years apart, independently.

Worth keeping rather than smoothing over: the name showed up with its full meaning already intact, and what exists today hasn't caught up to it. That's not a flaw in either one, just two different maturities sitting next to each other honestly.

Why Archemedeus

Arche — origin, beginning, first principle. The root of a world must precede everything that world contains.
Me — the self at the center of creation and responsibility. This is not an anonymous system; it has an author written into its name.
Deus — higher creation. Not worship, but the act of bringing a world into being: authorship and responsibility, not a claim of divinity.
Together: I started this, I stand responsible inside it, and I protect its continuity.

Why Arcaia

Arc echoes arch — beginning, origin, the supporting span that lets something greater rise.
Aia echoes Gaia — life, world, living ground, and the force that sustains a world.
Together: the root and life-support layer of Archemedeus, where models, tools, providers, retrieval, and execution get planted and grown.
Arcaia gives the system hands, roots, and soil. It is deliberately not the mind, and Core stays the interpreter of meaning and authority.

Language Design / Runtime

Zyeha

The origin language — where the doctrine is written down

A VM-based language with its own lexer, parser, compiler, and stack machine: the "own bytecode" path rather than a transpiler. Four traits carry it. Entities with behavior are core syntax, rather than a class plus an event bus you assemble yourself. Isolation is a one-word builtin that deep-copies across the boundary, so mutation genuinely cannot leak. Dialect-authoring is a first-class capability, which makes the language a substrate other languages get built on. And zero-silent-behavior is a totalizing rule: arithmetic overflow errors loudly, ambiguous heap-crossings refuse, and the parser collects every error instead of stopping at the first. Nothing else borrows all three of the first ones at once. Its North Star document is where the agnosticism doctrine on this page is actually written down.

Writing Tool — Light Suite

Tinta formerly Scriptorium

No account, no server, no network

A local-first novel-writing workspace, built because the other half of this page needed one, and now live. Chapters and scenes with cross-chapter drag, a corkboard of scene cards carrying goal, conflict, and outcome, an outline whose synopsis is assembled from those scenes, a timeline with real story dates, and a canvas graph over a generic entity-type engine, so a world can hold characters, places, magic systems, and calendars without the tool deciding which. [[Entity]] backlinks are computed on read rather than stored, so renaming a character never leaves a stale reference behind. Plain textarea over rich text, deliberately, because it stays trivial to store, diff, and export.

The shell was rebuilt from a prototype: navigation holds only the views that need the full centre, and anything contextual opens in the right rail instead. It ships in paper, void, and sepia themes, exports to DOCX, EPUB, and Markdown, and keeps full JSON backups.

Ward of Light

Device-local, cloud-free

A local-first TOTP authenticator (RFC 6238), built as a genuine drop-in for the mainstream authenticator apps and installable as a PWA on any platform that supports one. About

How the two sides connect

There's a full-time support role outside all of this, on different hours and a different discipline, with no overlap. Everything on this page got built around that schedule rather than instead of it. Formal schooling stopped at senior secondary; everything past that has been self-directed, which is most of why the work looks the way it does. Nothing here inherited a house style, for better and for worse.

The name came from an old handle before it meant anything: Arche-me-deus, roughly "I am the god of beginnings." In 【NEWLIGHT】 it isn't just a name, it's the power itself: the authority to hold a world's reality, past and present and future, in one hand. The real system picked up the same question almost by accident. If something is going to persist and grow, what actually gets to change it, and what has to stay constant no matter what? That's the throughline, whether it's a resident inside an AI system or a hero in a mythology that refuses to offer easy alignments.

My default setting is practical but deliberately overbuilt. I think best in motion, away from a keyboard, and most of what I read is progression fantasy: the shape of story where the system and the self are permanently renegotiating terms. Which is, more or less, the argument this entire page is making in a different register.

【NEWLIGHT】 started as a Discord server and became something I've led across four games and counting. The novel ends with a character splitting his soul into duality to become the fabric of the universe, which turned out to sit almost exactly on top of a real creation myth I only found afterward. I didn't plan that overlap. It just held.
